Privacy Policy (Attic Life)

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (English version added 2026-10-21). The Japanese version (privacy-policy.html) is the original; if the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

About the app

"Attic Life" (the "App", Japanese title: 屋根裏暮らし。) is a game app provided by En-Links LLC (合同会社エン・リンクス, the "Company").

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information only on your device and does not send it to the Company.

  • Game progress (the state of the house you are playing, attic furniture, carried-over resources, etc.)
  • Records (endings you have seen, scores, the minigame high score, etc.)
  • Settings (sound, language, etc.)

The App does not handle your name, email address, phone number, device location, contacts, photos, or the like. The App shows no ads, and it uses no ad, tracking, or gameplay-analytics tools. It does not collect an advertising ID.

When you uninstall the App, the information stored on your device is deleted. If Android backup is enabled in your device settings, this information may be included in the backup of your Google account. Backups are made as a Google service, and the Company cannot see their contents.

Purchasing the full version, and information sent to external services

Google Play handles the purchase process for the full version. Payment information (card numbers, etc.) is handled by Google, and the Company does not receive it.

To check purchase status and restore purchases, the App uses the service "RevenueCat" provided by RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). When the App starts, when you return to the App, and when you purchase or restore, the following information is sent to RevenueCat.

  • A random identifier created automatically by the App (it is not linked to your name or the like)
  • Purchase information (the product purchased, price and currency, the Google Play purchase token, date and time of purchase, etc.)
  • Device and app information (OS version, device model and manufacturer, language and region settings, app version, etc.)
  • Connection status (response times, types of errors, and a random identifier created each time the app starts. RevenueCat may collect these to investigate problems)
  • The IP address sent along with the connection (when the country of purchase is unknown, RevenueCat estimates the approximate country from the IP address. RevenueCat states that it does not store the IP address after this estimation)

This information is used only to check the purchase status of the full version, restore purchases, prevent fraudulent purchases, tally purchases by count and country, and investigate and improve purchase-related problems. In the RevenueCat dashboard, the Company can see the purchase information for each of the identifiers above.

Communication is encrypted (HTTPS). For how RevenueCat handles information, please see RevenueCat's privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). For information that Google handles during the purchase process, please see Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you have made a purchase, the identifier above may also be stored in your Google account's Block Store (Google's backup) so that your purchase status can carry over when you change devices or reinstall the App.

Apart from the above, the App does not communicate with external services on its own. When you tap a link inside the App, such as "Privacy Policy" in the settings, the page opens in your device's browser.

Retention period and deletion

Information sent to RevenueCat (purchase information, identifiers, device and app information, and the estimated country) is kept at RevenueCat, for checking and restoring purchases, until the Company deletes it. The retention period of connection status is set by RevenueCat. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete information that has already been sent. If you would like your purchase information deleted, please contact the email address below with your Google Play order number (a number starting with "GPA.") or the email address of the Google account you used to purchase. We will delete the corresponding information from RevenueCat. After deletion, restoring your purchase may no longer work correctly (the purchase itself on Google Play is not cancelled). If you keep using the App after deletion, the information above will be sent again to check the purchase status.

Information about people who have not purchased the full version (identifiers, device and app information, etc.) cannot be deleted individually, because the Company cannot identify whose it is. To stop the sending, please uninstall the App.

Information you give us when you contact us

Information you give us when you contact us, such as your email address, is used only to reply to your inquiry and to handle your requests (such as data deletion), and is deleted once handling is complete and it is no longer needed.

Provision to third parties

The Company does not sell information about the App to third parties. Except where required by law, it is not provided to anyone other than RevenueCat and Google above.
